# El Makina Asset Generation Brief

Use this brief for generated raster assets. The box cover is the source of truth:
pale paper, pastel blush/yellow wash, black ink typography, hot orange machine,
red side-panel accents, green CRT screens, purple monitor frames, teal screen glow,
small wires, knobs, and deliberately imperfect hand-drawn machinery.

## Role Notes

- Businesswoman: confident dealmaker with ledger, coin token, sharp jacket.
- TaxCollector: stern municipal clerk with stamp, receipt book, official seal shapes.
- Policewoman: investigative officer with magnifier, badge-like geometry, calm authority.
- Colonel: ceremonial officer with medals and cap, theatrical rather than realistic military.
- Thief: masked trickster with nimble pose, sly expression, no realistic weapon.
- Politician: smiling podium operator with papers and campaign pin shapes.
- Saboteur: replace direct extremist imagery with masked system-jammer, red warning wires, mischief and danger without real-world terror styling.
